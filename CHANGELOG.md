# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First release.

- Country highlights on satellite imagery (NASA Blue Marble, Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016) with tracing borders,
  glow, labels and a push-in or zoom-in camera; MP4, GIF, PNG and JPG in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5.
- Styles: solid tint, highlight (rest of the world grey), outline, flag.
- Paper basemap, sea grid, lens blur, neighbour borders, label animations, capital and city markers.
- Data maps with colour schemes, legends and value labels.
- Globe camera (3D Earth dive) and fly camera (country to country).
- States and provinces (Natural Earth admin-1) with their capitals.
- Disputed borders drawn from the highlighted country's official point of view by default.
- MCP server (`mapcast mcp`), Claude skill with 27 worked examples, Claude Code plugin.
