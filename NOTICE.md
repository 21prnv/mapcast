# Credits and data licences

## Map data
- **Country borders:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain. Downloaded on first use from
  [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).
- **Satellite imagery (zoom 8 and below):** NASA Blue Marble, public domain, served by [NASA GIBS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/gibs).
- **Satellite imagery (closer than zoom 8):** [Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016](https://s2maps.eu) by EOX IT Services GmbH
  (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. Credit is required when this source is used;
  `mapcast` prints the exact credit line after every render.

- **Capitals and cities:** Natural Earth populated places, public domain.
- **Flags:** artwork from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT), downloaded on first use. National flag
  designs are public domain.

## Prior art
These MIT-licensed projects shaped the design:
- [rgiq2024/Map-Animator](https://github.com/rgiq2024/Map-Animator): boundary effects (trace, fill, glow, darken outside) and frame-exact tile loading.
- [SpaceZephyr/map-motion](https://github.com/SpaceZephyr/map-motion): the "agent writes JSON, a fixed engine renders it" approach.
- [iart-ai/map-animation-skills](https://github.com/iart-ai/map-animation-skills): packaging map animation as an agent skill.
